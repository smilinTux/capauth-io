Try It Live

Experience sovereign identity in your browser. No servers. No accounts. Pure client-side cryptography.

1

Generate Your Keypair

Create a cryptographic identity using your browser's WebCrypto API. The private key never leaves your device.

2

Challenge-Response Auth

A verifier sends a random challenge. You sign it with your private key. They verify with your public key. No passwords. No servers.

3

Issue Capability Token

Create a signed capability token granting specific permissions. Self-contained, independently verifiable, revocable.
